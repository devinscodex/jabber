What is Jabber?
Jabber is Org's own framework for running local AI models through a web interface. It connects to models hosted on our own Leviathan server instead of a public AI service, so conversations stay on our own hardware and are never sent out to a third party. For some users, it can also connect to Sage, Wrike, and company files to help answer specific work questions.
